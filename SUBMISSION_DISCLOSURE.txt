SUGAM: Simplified Unified Guidance and Assistance for Mobility

An independent prototype for simplifying natural-language railway booking workflows.

IMPORTANT DISCLOSURE:
SUGAM is not an official IRCTC or government system and is not affiliated with IRCTC.
The prototype is designed for hackathon demonstration and uses synthetic/mock railway
inventory, passenger records, payment and PNR generation. It does not perform live
ticket booking or interfere with production government systems.
